# 0003 Ball

**Status:** implemented
**Date:** 2026-09-19

## Goal

A rally that reads fairly: the ball bounces where it looks like it should, and
where it hits a paddle changes where it goes.

## Behavior

The ball moves at 0.75 window widths per second, multiplied by delta time.

**Paddles.** A hit is counted when the ball's drawn square overlaps a paddle. The
ball is then placed against the face of the paddle it hit, so it can't register a
second hit on the next frame, and it leaves at an angle set by where it struck:
dead center sends it straight out, and nearer an end sends it further off square.
The bounce queues a sound.

**Top and bottom.** The ball bounces when its edge reaches the window's edge, not
its center, and its vertical direction flips.

**Left and right.** When the ball's center leaves the window, the player on the
other side scores. At five points that player wins and the game ends. Otherwise
the ball is served again from the middle, toward whoever conceded.

## Acceptance criteria

- The ball moves by its velocity scaled by delta time. — `system::tests::ball_moves_with_delta_time`
- Hitting a paddle sends the ball back the other way. — `system::tests::paddle_bounce_reverses_the_ball`
- After a bounce the ball rests outside the paddle. — `system::tests::paddle_bounce_moves_the_ball_clear`
- Where the ball strikes a paddle changes its angle. — `util::tests::bounce_angle_depends_on_where_it_hits`
- The ball bounces off the top at its edge. — `system::tests::ball_bounces_off_the_top`
- The ball bounces off the bottom at its edge. — `system::tests::ball_bounces_off_the_bottom`
- Leaving the right side scores for player one. — `system::tests::ball_past_the_right_scores_for_player_one`
- Leaving the left side scores for player two. — `system::tests::ball_past_the_left_scores_for_player_two`
- Five points ends the game. — `system::tests::fifth_point_ends_the_game`
- A serve starts in the middle, toward whoever conceded. — `system::tests::serve_starts_in_the_middle`

### Verified by hand

- Each bounce plays one sound, not a stutter. — run pong and rally.

## Out of scope

Spin, speeding the ball up during a rally, and any bounce off the top or bottom of
a paddle rather than its face.
